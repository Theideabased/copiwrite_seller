# Copiwrite marketing strategy

## Positioning decision

Copiwrite should not sell “words” or generic digital marketing. Those categories are easy to compare on price and increasingly easy to replace with general-purpose AI. The stronger position is a compact growth studio that connects four things clients usually buy separately:

1. Offer and audience clarity
2. Conversion copy for websites and landing pages
3. Campaign and lifecycle content
4. Sales enablement assets that help teams close

The public promise is: **Turn what you sell into words people act on.**

## Ideal customers

Primary: founder-led B2B software and service companies with a working offer but unclear positioning, inconsistent campaigns, or a website that fails to create qualified conversations.

Secondary: small marketing and sales teams preparing a launch, repositioning, or new outbound motion without enough senior messaging capacity.

Good-fit signals:

- The product works, but prospects still ask what it does.
- The founder is rewriting every important piece of copy.
- Marketing and sales describe the offer differently.
- Traffic exists, but enquiry quality is weak.
- A launch or website redesign needs a coherent message system.

## Market observation

Competitors increasingly bundle AI writing, templates, SEO, and content volume. That makes speed and output quantity table stakes. Copiwrite should compete on judgment: research, positioning, consistency across the funnel, and sales usefulness.

## Message hierarchy

1. Outcome: clearer value and more decisive buyer action.
2. Mechanism: research → message system → conversion assets → iteration.
3. Deliverables: positioning, pages, emails, campaigns, decks, and sales collateral.
4. Trust: no invented numbers; show process, scope, and relevant work as evidence becomes available.
5. CTA: a short project brief, followed by a fit conversation.

## Offer architecture

### Message Sprint

For a new offer or repositioning. Produces audience insight, category framing, value proposition, proof plan, voice guidance, and a messaging document.

### Conversion Build

For a website or campaign launch. Produces the message strategy plus page architecture, landing-page copy, lifecycle emails, and sales follow-up assets.

### Embedded Growth Partner

For ongoing execution. Provides monthly campaign planning, content creation, testing ideas, sales enablement, and message refinement.

## Recommended launch funnel

1. Publish this marketing site with one conversion event: project enquiry.
2. Add 2–3 detailed case studies once real client evidence is available.
3. Publish one high-intent article each month around positioning, website conversion, launch messaging, and sales-content alignment.
4. Repurpose each article into a founder LinkedIn post, a short email, and a diagnostic checklist.
5. Track source, service interest, budget band, qualified-call rate, proposal rate, and closed revenue.

## First 90 days

- Weeks 1–2: launch the site, configure enquiry delivery, analytics, Search Console, and a privacy-friendly event tracker.
- Weeks 3–6: publish the first case study and two diagnostic articles; start founder-led outreach using a useful teardown rather than a generic pitch.
- Weeks 7–12: review enquiry quality, refine the hero and service language, and add pricing guidance if poor-fit leads are consuming time.

## Evidence still needed

- Confirmed client list and approved logos
- Before/after examples
- Conversion or revenue results with measurement context
- Geographic focus and preferred industries
- Pricing floors and delivery capacity

Do not add these to the website until they are verified.
