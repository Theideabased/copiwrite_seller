# Brand: Copiwrite

Copiwrite builds AI sales automation systems for U.S. service businesses and SMBs. The main website focuses on lead response, qualification, follow-up, recovery, booking, and human handoff.

## B2B homepage direction (September 2026)

The homepage uses a white background, charcoal text (#15251d), green actions (#17653f), soft green surfaces (#f3f7f4), and the existing zero-network sans-serif stack. Its scoped tokens live in `app/automation.css`. This direction supersedes the original editorial palette on the homepage only; existing WhatsApp product and payment pages retain their themes.

Use short American English, concrete sales outcomes, and clearly labeled example workflows. Never invent client results, integrations, or testimonials. The main CTA is “See How It Works”; demo requests go to the contact form, not a checkout.

The stacks are defined in `app/globals.css`. They require no external font download during development, build, or runtime.

## Gradients

One subtle warm background gradient is available for the hero. There is no accent gradient; a flat primary color keeps calls to action precise.

## Tone and voice

Use short, declarative sentences and tangible outcomes. Say what gets clearer, faster, or easier to sell. Prefer “landing-page message” over “innovative solution.”

Avoid agency clichés: revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, bespoke solutions, and 360-degree marketing. Never invent client results or imply performance without evidence.

Voice example: “You know the value. We make your market see it.”

## Usage

- Use the warm paper base and ink typography as the default.
- Reserve orange for actions, focus, and small moments of emphasis.
- Use generous whitespace, strong editorial hierarchy, and asymmetric compositions.
- Do not use violet-blue gradients, glass cards, stock photography, or uniform feature-card grids.
