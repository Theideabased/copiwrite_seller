## 1. DESIGN FOR THREE TYPES OF VISITORS

The page must convert three visitor behaviors:

### WATCHERS

People who land on the page and watch the main sales video.

The video should be sufficient to explain the problem, teach useful information, introduce the implementation system and make the offer.

### READERS

People who don't watch the video but read the sales copy.

The written sales page must independently make the complete sales argument.

Do NOT make understanding the offer dependent on watching the video.

### SCANNERS

People who immediately scroll down looking for:

* what the product is,
* what they get,
* proof,
* price,
* and whether it is worth buying.

The page must remain persuasive even when someone only reads major headlines, highlighted statements, visual frameworks and offer sections.

---

## 2. DO NOT FORCE VIDEO CONSUMPTION

Never:

* lock scrolling,
* hide the price until the video finishes,
* require video completion,
* disable page navigation,
* force users to watch before purchasing.

Allow visitors to freely scroll.

Some people scrolling directly to the price may have high buying intent.
